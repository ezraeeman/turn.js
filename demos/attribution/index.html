Prototyping the Future of Attribution

Most current discussions about AI and publishers remain trapped in a binary debate between extraction and blocking.

But AI-native interfaces create the possibility for entirely new forms of attribution and value exchange. If assistants and agents become the dominant interface to information, publishers need more than citations at the bottom of generated answers.

  • ✓persistent identity
  • ✓transparent provenance
  • ✓measurable contribution
  • ✓conversational presence
  • ✓participation in the economic layer of the AI web

This is why publishers should actively prototype possible futures with companies like Microsoft, Google and OpenAI — not simply to defend traffic, but to redesign how trust, visibility and value work in AI-mediated environments.
