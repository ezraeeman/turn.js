Executive summary

The group converged on a strategy that treats AI interfaces as both a distribution opportunity and a structural risk. The organization should require visible attribution, protect the audience relationship and prototype publisher-owned conversational news experiences.

Main themes

  • Loss of direct audience relationship
  • Attribution and visibility in AI interfaces
  • Dependency on big tech platforms
  • Need for publisher-owned AI experiences
  • Public value and trustworthy information

Key tensions

  • Open access versus content protection
  • Collaboration with AI platforms versus strategic dependency
  • Innovation speed versus editorial safeguards

Decisions made

  • Attribution is a strategic requirement for AI distribution.
  • Protecting the audience relationship is a criterion for platform partnerships.

Open questions

  • What should remain uniquely owned?
  • How should user benefit from summaries be measured?
  • What licensing model preserves public value?

Suggested next steps

  • Define attribution requirements for AI partners.
  • Run a two-week prototype of a publisher-owned conversational news experience.
  • Map licensing and crawler policy options.
